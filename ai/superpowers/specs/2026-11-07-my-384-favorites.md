# Spec: favorite quotes (MY-384)

Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-my-384-favorites.md`.

## Star

- Every quote card and the quote page get a third icon button after copy and share. It is a toggle:
  `aria-pressed` is `false` or `true`, the accessible name is «Add to favorites» when not saved and
  «Remove from favorites» when saved. Pressed shows a filled star in the accent colour, not pressed an
  outline; focus ring, 44 px target and Enter / Space like the other two.
- Clicking saves the quote (id, text, author, categories) at the top of the list, or removes it. All
  cards of the same quote on the page change at once; the state survives a reload and follows changes
  made in another tab. No toast.
- The star stays in read-only mode.

## Storage

- `localStorage` key `quotes:favorites`, a list of quotes, newest first, one entry per id.
- Missing storage, a throwing storage, broken JSON or a value that is not a list: no favorites, no error.
  An entry of the wrong shape is skipped, the valid ones stay.
- When saving fails (private mode, quota) the star still works until the page closes.
- The server renders every star not pressed; the saved state appears after hydration with no warning.

## Page `/favorites`

- Title «Favorites», `noindex`, canonical `/favorites`. A heading «Favorites» and the saved quotes as the
  usual cards (text truncated at 200 characters with a link to the quote, author, copy, share, star,
  categories), newest first, in the grid of `Quotes`.
- No saved quotes: «No favorite quotes yet.» and a link «Search quotes» to `/search`.
- Until the client has read the storage nothing is shown under the heading (no flash of the empty
  state). The page makes no API request.
- Removing a quote with the star removes its card at once.

## Menu

- «Favorites» between «Search» and «Create New» in the desktop row and the mobile menu; marked current
  on `/favorites`; present in read-only mode.

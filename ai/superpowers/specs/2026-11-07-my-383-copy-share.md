# Spec: copy and share a quote (MY-383)

Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-my-383-copy-share.md`.

## Buttons

- Every quote card (home, random, search) and the quote page show two icon buttons, «Copy quote» and
  «Share quote» (the accessible names), grouped in a row. The card's links and layout are unchanged.
- Both are real buttons, reachable with Tab, activated with Enter and Space, with a visible focus ring in
  both themes, and about 44 px touch targets. Both stay visible in read-only mode (they only read).

## Copy

- Writes `"text" — author` (straight quotes, em dash) to the clipboard, the full text, never the card's
  truncated one.
- Success: a toast «Quote copied to clipboard.»
- No clipboard API, or the write is refused: an error toast «Could not copy to the clipboard.»

## Share

- With `navigator.share`: opens the system share sheet with `title` «Quote by <author>», `text` the same
  `"text" — author`, and `url` the absolute address of `/quotes/:id` on the current origin.
- Without `navigator.share` (most desktops): copies that link and shows «Link copied to clipboard.»; if the
  clipboard fails, the same error toast as copy.
- The user closing the share sheet (`AbortError`) shows nothing. Any other share failure: an error toast
  «Could not share the quote.»

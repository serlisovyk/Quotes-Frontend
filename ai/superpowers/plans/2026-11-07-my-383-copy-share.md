# Plan: copy and share a quote (MY-383)

Branch `my-383-copy-share`. Behavior: `ai/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-my-383-copy-share.md`.

## Author's decisions (2026-11-07)

- Copy and share on the card and on the quote page; the copied format is left to the plan.

## Decisions in this plan

- **Copied text:** `"text" — author`: straight quotes and an em dash, plain text that pastes well anywhere.
  The share payload reuses it as `text`, with `title` `Quote by <author>` and `url` the quote link.
- **Link:** built on the client from `window.location.origin` and `ROUTES.QUOTES`, not `SITE_URL`, so it
  is right on every deployment and in dev.
- **Errors as toasts:** the issue asks for an error toast. `toasts-and-inline-errors` keeps mutation
  failures inline next to their form; a clipboard failure has no field to sit beside and nothing to fix,
  so it is a toast. A short record states this refinement.
- **Read-only:** copy and share only read, so they stay (`read-only-mode`).
- **Layers:** utils `formatQuoteText`, `createQuoteShareData` (pure); hook `useQuoteActions` owns the
  clipboard, `navigator.share` and the toasts; components `QuoteActions` (client, the row),
  `QuoteActionButton` (one icon button), `QuoteActionIcon` (svg from `QUOTE_ACTION_ICON_PATHS`). Messages,
  labels, icon paths and classes are constants. A later button is one more entry in the list the row maps
  over, no new structure.
- **Placement:** the card gets a right-aligned row between author and tags; the quote page a centred row
  between author and tags. Callers pass `id`, `text`, `author`.
- No `navigator.canShare` branch and no fallback beyond the link copy: not asked for.
